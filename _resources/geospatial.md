Geospatial Technology encompassess a wide array of disciplines and professions, offering benefical applications no matter what career you are pursuing. 

## What is Geospatial Science? 

Geospatial science looks at relationships over space and time. It can be used for various applications such as 

- Measuring Distance Between ciites or states
- Looking at an areas population 
- Identifying the Vegetation in an Area
- Looking at changes in ice/snowmelt over time 
- Following where animal species move in an area throughout time 

These are only a few applications of Geospatial Science. There are many more that our CSU courses as well as online resources can help you learn about!
